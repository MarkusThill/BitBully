# References {#references}

The following resources directly inspired BitBully's implementation. They
are excellent reading for anyone interested in solved games or strong
search heuristics. Citations resolve into the auto-generated
[Bibliography](citelist.html) page.

## Foundational papers

- L. V. Allis, *A Knowledge-based Approach of Connect-Four — The Game is
  Solved: White Wins*, Master's thesis, Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, 1988.
  @cite allis88
- A. Plaat, J. Schaeffer, W. Pijls, A. de Bruin,
  *Best-First Fixed-Depth Minimax Algorithms*, Artificial Intelligence
  87 (1996), 255&ndash;293 &mdash; the original MTD(*f*) paper.
  @cite plaat96
- J. Schaeffer, *The History Heuristic and Alpha-Beta Search Enhancements
  in Practice*, IEEE PAMI, 11 (1989). @cite schaeffer89

## Code that influenced BitBully
